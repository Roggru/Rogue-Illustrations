King Osteon

I knew an erstwhile greatness. A time with such
A golden bliss and reddened fury.
Behind my steps, an empire grew
And yet courted every soul worth more than stars.
A hunger hid within me, unending, and unlike
All others that saw a tide of battle.
Naught a crown nor call happened upon
A moving mountain, a rushing river, a biting blade.
An anger as a babe, that knew not the age of defeat
But the blood of a manifold breast.

But one warrior stands before me, defiant to my power.
To my wake of greatness.
And to my words of greeting, he says to me in a tongue foreign,
Even more unlike me. Full of melancholy, but so too of peace.
A whispering silence and a hidden, unreachable glare
Says he is more incapable of fear, of cowardice, of
Death.

His dancing was minimal, his technique—of a fledgling.
I entertained it; enjoyed it, that we, for a time,
Were of the same cloth.
He flailed his meek sword and drew a light from within my skin.
Something more than red drips at the tip of the sword.
Of a past, noble and holy, and all its days it had lived.

I dare none to come in aide, though they would protest.
To see me at my heel and knee, and before a nightmare
Of groveling, pleading, and begging. Never.
At my moment of weakness.
No rope had coiled at my throat, yet I hung.
No fire had swallowed me there, yet I burned.
No chain had bound me, yet I was at a mercy for a time,
That demanded a humiliation which was taken.
I cannot see his eyes beneath that black visor, though so close
As to an embrace at his breast, whilst he lets me go
Of my living.

Then, only then, did I understand the tongue he spoke in.
Yet still I resisted. I fought, and fought
And pushed away the loss. I had not, for as long as
My greatness can live.
I beheld a wailing, sobbing tragedy. And like a lover,
An ethereal dress wrapped at my wants and dreams
Made of curses and regrets.
I do not think he took—nor understood
To my departing words
As I truly meant them.

Even so, now, I will have an eternity to enact my wrath.
I am boundless, and my thoughts have become neverending.
Neverending.

Neverending.

Neverending.
